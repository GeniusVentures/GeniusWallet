## NEW - raised by Jakub 2026-07-31 at the end of the walk, NOT yet planned

### 1. Order rows must match Transactions-tab rows

The Buy GNUS "Your orders" rail currently renders `_OrderRailRow`, private to
`lib/screens/banxa_buy_screen.dart:928`. Jakub: *"chcialbym, zeby mialy one taka sama strukture i
layout oraz wszystko praktycznie, jak transakcje, ktore sa w Transaction Tabie. To musza byc te same
komponenty praktycznie."*

The transactions-tab row is `TransactionRow` at `lib/dashboard/home/widgets/transaction_displays.dart:241`.

Note the existing doc comment on `_OrderRailRow` explicitly rejects reusing `OrderCard` because that
one carries up to three action buttons - so a previous session already reasoned about reuse here and
picked a bespoke row. That reasoning has to be revisited against `TransactionRow`, not against
`OrderCard`. Expect a shape mismatch: an `Order` is not a `Transaction` (see item 2's finding).

### 2. Tapping an order must open the transaction-details drawer

Jakub: *"jesli klikasz na Transactions w sekcji Your Orders, to powinien Ci sie otworzyc drawer z
Transaction details, identyczne jak mamy w Transactions tabie. Ewentualnie respektuj te informacje,
ktore sa dostarczone przez system."*

**The blocking finding, already established today** (todo filed at
`.planning/todos/pending/2026-07-31-banxa-orders-never-enter-the-transaction-store.md`): Banxa orders
never become `Transaction`s. `TransactionType.purchase` is assigned in exactly two places, both dev
fixtures. Nothing under `lib/banxa/` constructs a `Transaction` or calls `addTransaction`.

So the details drawer cannot simply be handed an order - either the drawer is generalised over a
smaller interface, or an `Order` is mapped into a `Transaction`-shaped view model. Jakub's
"ewentualnie respektuj te informacje, ktore sa dostarczone przez system" is the licence to show fewer
fields where Banxa provides fewer. **He explicitly invited questions on this one.**

### 3. Swap flip button is not vertically centred once tokens are chosen

At rest the gradient flip control sits exactly between the two cards. Once a token is selected and an
amount typed, the "You Pay" card grows (a `$0.00` sub-line and a `1 ETH / MAX` row appear) and the
control stays put instead of re-centring on the new boundary.

Start at `lib/squid_router/swap_screen.dart:676-682` - there is already a comment there about
centring the flip control on a `Stack`'s bounding box, which is the mechanism that stops working when
the cards become unequal.
