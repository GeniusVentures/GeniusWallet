The composition is settled and is not reopened here. Two controls, top-right, inside title: a 44 px wallet icon, space6 = 12, a 44 px hamburger. 100.00 px against the shipped pill's 223.94. This sketch changes only the skin - fill, edge, gradient - and every variant carries the same geometry to the pixel so the comparison is about the treatment and nothing else.

One constraint decides most of it, and it is not taste. surfaceMenu #171A21 on surfaceElevated #0C0E14 measures 1.11:1. The fill genuinely cannot identify the control, which is exactly why the shipped pill puts borderControl (white 36%, 3.30:1) on its edge - the boundary carries WCAG 1.4.11 alone. So "no border" is not a look, it is a question: what identifies this as a control at 3:1 instead? Every variant below prints its own measured answer and a pass or a fail. Two of them fail and are shown anyway.

The wallet-name states are here because the monogram behaves differently in each, and one of them is a defect case: ADDRESS-SHAPED NAME is a wallet saved with an address typed into the free-text name field at import_security_screen.dart:236. Sketch 181 shipped a monogram rule that got its own ordering wrong on the first pass and rendered that wallet as 21 - an address fragment. The rule here tests address-shaped first; the live output is printed under every phone, so the address test runs on every render. 44pt targets overlays the real 44 x 44 hit box on both controls - the point of the toggle is that the box is identical in all six variants and only the paint inside it moves.
