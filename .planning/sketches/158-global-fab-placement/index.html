The floating swap button: where it sits, and what it is made of

Jakub, 2026-07-27: "kolko - zmiana lokacji bo gap od dolu i od prawej nie jest rowny, zaproponuj design; HTML raz zrob jeden gdzie tylko obramowka jest gradient". Two separate questions, both answered here: placement (four options, top block) and skin (four options, in the toolbar - switch it and every window below re-skins at once).

The cause measured global_swap_fab_host.dart:124-128 is literally Positioned(right: GeniusWalletConsts.space10, bottom: 80 + bottomInset). space10 is 20. So the right gap is 20px and the bottom gap is 80px - a 4:1 asymmetry, and it is not a rounding slip, it is two different numbers chosen for two different reasons.
Why 80 The comment says it: "Clears the 60px bottom nav (+ safe-area) on the main shell". That bottom nav is _MobileTabBar, and it is mounted only by MobileOverlay (responsive_overlay.dart:486). DesktopOverlay has no bottom bar at all - the navigation is the top navbar. So on the desktop app the FAB is clearing a bar that is not there. 60 of those 80 pixels are avoidance of nothing.
Second effect The two glows (gw_swap_fab.dart:56-69) are offset (-2, 6) and (2, 6) with a 24px blur, so the button's optical mass hangs about 30px below its box. That does not rescue the gap - it means the visible shape is roughly 50px off the floor and 20px off the wall, still 2.5:1, while the glow itself gets clipped on the right at only 20px of clearance.

The gradient-outline skin, in detail

All four skins at 1:1 on the three canvases the FAB actually floats over. The middle one is the dark page canvas (surfaceBase #0B0D12), which is where it lives most of the time.

Over surfaceBase and next to a card, so the ring can be judged against a real hairline.
Over a card (surfaceElevated) - the FAB overlaps panels on the dashboard.
Over the mesh canvas - the worst case for a see-through ring.

The one technical thing this cannot be built without

A BorderSide takes a single Color. There is no Flutter border that is a gradient, which is exactly why this project already built GWFocusRing during Phase 20. A gradient-outline FAB is therefore not a border - it is a gradient-filled circle with an inset circle of canvas colour on top: 

Container(                       // the ring
  width: 56, height: 56,
  decoration: BoxDecoration(
    gradient: GeniusWalletGradient.brandCta,
    shape: BoxShape.circle,
  ),
  padding: const EdgeInsets.all(1.5),
  child: Container(              // the hole
    decoration: BoxDecoration(
      color: gw.surfaceBase,     // S1 - or a brand tint for S2
      shape: BoxShape.circle,
    ),
    child: Icon(Icons.swap_vert_rounded, color: ..., size: 26),
  ),
)

watch The hole is painted with a specific surface colour, so the FAB stops being canvas-agnostic: over the mesh and over a card it will read as a disc of the wrong shade unless the fill is either translucent (S3) or deliberately tinted (S2). That is the real trade this question is about, and it is why the three canvases above are drawn.

contrast The glyph colour cannot stay textOnBrand #000B18 - that is a colour for sitting on the gradient. On the dark canvas brandSecondary #2BF5B4 is ~12:1 and fine; in light mode it is ~1.6:1 and fails, so the light branch needs the darkened #0A6885 the project already uses for brand-on-surface text.
