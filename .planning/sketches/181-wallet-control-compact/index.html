Jakub, later the same day, a new direction: "sprobuj w prawym gornym rogu zrobic ikonke walletu, czy cos podobnego w krotszej formie, i obok tego hamburger menu. I wtedy, zamiast 'more' na samym dole, moglibysmy dac na przyklad 'news'."

The complaint is width, and it is arithmetically correct. At 390pt the shipped pill occupies up to 223.94 px - 57.4% of the bar, and 65.5% of the 342 px the title Row actually gets. Every scheme below is judged first on how many pixels it occupies at 390pt, printed under its phone, and then on what you can still tell without tapping. Tapping any control opens the sheet it claims to open.
