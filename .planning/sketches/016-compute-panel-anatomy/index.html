The unit clash

Flip Show unit clash. The left card's 1,204.50 GNUS and Assets' $312.40 are the same money, counted twice, 12 px apart — one polled from the SDK every 10 s (genius_balance_display.dart:56), the other computed from CoinGecko every 60 s (coins_screen.dart:243). They will routinely disagree. B3 is the only round-2 variant that resolves it, by adopting the same unit; C removes the duplicate entirely.

Decision — B2 · Twin tiles ★

Chosen 2026-07-22. Both concerns get a container and read as siblings of equal rank — which is the honest description of what this card now is: a balance readout and a compute node, not one thing with an appendix.

B2 did not fit when it was proposed. At 244 px base plus a 38 px Not linked state it hit 282 px against a 276 px budget — a real overflow in the most common non-happy state. The fix is not to shave padding: the centred "why" row under the CTA was merged into the status sub-line, so the reason and its remedy now share one line ("Not the wallet linked to SGNUS · switch wallet ›"). That removes 17 px of text plus a 6 px gap from four of the six states, and it is better copy — the explanation and the way out are no longer separated by a button.

Result: B2's worst state is now 261 px — +15 px of headroom. It fits, but it is the tightest of the four by a wide margin. Treat that as a standing constraint: anything added to the compute block later breaks B2 first. The +12.4 GNUS earned line is exactly such an addition — it is already excluded from B2's measurement and stays a stretch item, gated on a mint/job-reward aggregate that no current API exposes.

Carried over from B4: the 2 px brand cap, applied to the balance tile only while a job is running — a quiet, non-textual signal that the number is about to change. B4's raised surface is not carried over; two elevations inside one card fight each other.

On the unit clash: B2 keeps it. 1,204.50 GNUS here and $312.40 in Assets remain the same money in two units — mitigated only by the ≈ $312.40 subline making the relationship explicit. B3 was the variant that resolved it outright and it was not chosen; if the disagreement between the 10 s SDK poll and the 60 s CoinGecko poll ever becomes visible in practice, B3 is the fallback.
