What colour is a drawer?

Jakub, 2026-07-27: "Ten drawer jest ok, ale chodzi mi o kolory... poprobuj cos, wydaje mi sie ze ciemniejsze trzeba, bardziej kompatybilne z reszta". The shape of this drawer was settled by sketch 063-A and shipped; nothing structural moves here. Six colour schemes for the same panel - switch them in the toolbar and watch the app behind at the same time.

The palette Four dark surface tokens exist, and the drawer uses the lightest one.
surfaceSunken#06080C
surfaceBase · the page#0B0D12
surfaceElevated · every card#0C0E14
surfaceMenu · the drawer#171A21
Finding 1 measured responsive_drawer.dart:139 paints the whole panel gw.surfaceMenu (#171A21). Every card in the app is surfaceElevated #0C0E14 and the page is surfaceBase #0B0D12. So the drawer is roughly two full steps lighter than anything it opens over, and it is the only large surface in the app at that value. That is the "niekompatybilne z reszta", stated in hex.
Finding 2 1.00:1 The custom-value field is painted the same colour as the panel it sits on: swap_settings_drawer.dart:193 passes background: gw.surfaceMenu to GWFocusRing, and the panel is already surfaceMenu. The field's fill contrast against its own canvas is exactly 1.00:1 - it is not a box, it is a hairline. On the screenshot the input reads as an outline floating in the panel, and that is literally what it is.
Finding 3 1.4.11 That hairline is borderSubtle = white at 12%, which composites to #33363C over #171A21 and measures 1.43:1 against it. When a hairline is the only thing identifying an input, WCAG 1.4.11 wants 3:1. Working backwards on this canvas: white at 30% is the first step that clears it (3.0:1 against #0C0E14). Every scheme below except 0 uses 30% on the field only - card hairlines stay at 12%, because those are decorative.
Not a fix Two dark fills can never carry an input boundary on their own: #171A21 on #0C0E14 is 1.11:1 and #06080C on #0C0E14 is 1.04:1. On a dark canvas the border does the work and the fill only sets the mood. That is why the recommendation below is a pair - a darker panel and a stronger field edge - not either alone.
