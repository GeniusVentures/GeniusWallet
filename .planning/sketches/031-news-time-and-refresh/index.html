031 · News — timestamp & refresh

Two News questions, real components at 1:1. Timestamp = how relativeTime ("3 hours ago", news_article.dart) reads on a card. Refresh = the header control (today a ghost GWButton). Toggle Dark/Light top-right.

1 · Timestamp — how “3 hours ago” reads

Shown on the real card meta line. The value comes from relativeTime; only the presentation changes. A recency feed lives or dies on scannability — the eye should catch “how old” without reading a sentence.

T2 chosen — now the colour: green vs gradient

The tradeoff to decide: green currently means “fresh (<1h)”. If EVERY timestamp is green/gradient, that signal is gone — colour no longer tells age. Two ways to keep both a lively brand accent AND the meaning are C3/C4 below. Gradient text is a ShaderMask over the brand green→blue (mint on dark).

2 · Refresh control

In the header row (title · search · refresh). Today it is a labelled ghost button. A news feed refresh is stronger when it also says when it last updated — otherwise the button is a guess.
