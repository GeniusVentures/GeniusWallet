194 - the "Updated" stamp and its icon

Jakub, 2026-08-09, on the shipped Crypto News scheme C: "one last thing about Crypto News. Please think about how we can present this in the updated form, including that icon. I have the impression it looks weird, so prepare three or four designs for how to improve it there." Read here as the header's freshness control - the Updated 2m ago stamp, its refresh glyph, and where that pair belongs on a phone (_UpdatedStamp, crypto_news_screen.dart:241-297). This is an interpretation, not a fact; the only other icon on the page is the broken-photo placeholder, and scheme D covers that in case the reading is wrong. Every scheme renders all four states the stamp actually has: cold start, fresh, 3h old, and a press that fetches nothing because the 2-minute cache is still warm.

T - as shipped  today

Exactly what is on the phone now. Press the refresh glyph in any state and watch what happens to the page under it.

T - what the pair costs, measured

The trailing is a Row of a bodySm label, space4, and an IconButton with an 18px glyph (crypto_news_screen.dart:274-295). GWPageHeader puts a trailing beside the whole identity block, not inside the title line (gw_page_header.dart:212-239), so the trailing sets the header row's height.

Three things that are true and are not obvious

1. The button's own reason does not exist on a phone. Its comment says it is there because "pull-to-refresh alone is unreachable with a mouse" (crypto_news_screen.dart:166-169). There is no mouse here, and RefreshIndicator IS mounted on this page (crypto_news_screen.dart:210). On the phone the glyph is a second door to a room that already has one.

2. Its consistency argument is stale. The code says the bare icon matches "the Markets header's trailing action, the magnifying glass" (crypto_news_screen.dart:286-287). Markets today is const GWPageHeader(title: "Markets") with no trailing at all (markets_screen.dart:114), and neither Transactions (:114) nor Assets (:395) has one either. News is the only left-aligned content tab with anything in its header trailing.

3. Pressing it blanks the page. _retryNews is setState(_load), which assigns a NEW future (crypto_news_screen.dart:74-86). FutureStateWidget returns Center(child: Loading()) for ConnectionState.waiting (custom_future_builder.dart:28-29), so the hero and all 29 rows are replaced by a spinner - including on the warm-cache path, which returns the same 30 articles it already had.

And the stamp can lie

_load stamps _lastUpdated = DateTime.now() in whenComplete. The feed returns the cache untouched for 2 minutes (coin_telegraph_api.dart:12, :27-33), so a press inside that window resets the label to Updated now having fetched nothing. Try Warm cache below and press.

The weirdness is real and it is structural. A 40px tap target that duplicates a gesture, on the one header in the app that carries a control, beside a label that can be wrong.
The 30-second ticker

_UpdatedStampState runs Timer.periodic(30s) whose only job is to re-render its own label (crypto_news_screen.dart:251-268). The label can change at most once a minute, so at least half of those ticks are no-ops; after the first hour it is 119 out of 120. It is cheap - one setState on a two-child Row - and it is correctly scoped to the stamp rather than the magazine. It is not the problem, and three of the four schemes keep it.

A - the stamp without the button  recommended

The glyph is gone on the phone; the sentence stays. Refresh is the pull gesture that was already mounted. Hover the top of the list to see the pull zone, or use the button below.

A - delete the door that leads where the other one goes

One condition on one widget: the IconButton is mounted only when GeniusBreakpoints.useDesktopLayout(context), which is the same test that already decides where the search field lives (crypto_news_screen.dart:151-182). Desktop keeps its mouse-reachable refresh, which is the only place its comment was ever true. The phone keeps the sentence.

The header is the only place that survives every state

This is the argument that decided the ranking. Expanded(FutureStateWidget(...)) replaces the whole body during the first fetch, on an error and on an empty feed (crypto_news_screen.dart:183-226). The header sits OUTSIDE it. On a cold start the page's only words are the title and Updating… - which is exactly why the stamp was made unconditional in the first place ("so the header never looks empty / jumps", crypto_news_screen.dart:277-280). Any scheme that moves the stamp into the list loses it in three states. Switch this phone to Cold start and then switch C to the same state.

What it does not fix on its own

Two defects are independent of placement and A should carry both fixes with it:

  • Read the fetch time, not the settle time. The label should come from the cache's own timestamp (coinTelegraphTimestampBox, coin_telegraph_api.dart:97), so a warm-cache refresh cannot reset it to "now". Press refresh here in the Warm cache state: the label correctly stays at 1m ago.
  • Refresh in place. Keep the last list on screen while refetching instead of swapping the future and blanking to a spinner. With the stamp reading Updating… the page already has its progress indicator.
★ Recommendation. It removes precisely the thing that looks weird, it costs zero new components, it makes the News header identical to Transactions, Markets and Assets, and it keeps the freshness fact in the one region of the page that exists in every state. The 30s ticker stays; it is 60 no-op rebuilds an hour on a two-widget row.
The honest objection

On the phone the only refresh is a gesture with no visible affordance. That is already true of every other scroll surface in the app, and the label answers the question the button was usually pressed to answer ("is this current?") without pressing anything.

B - one object, not two  runner-up

The stamp and the glyph become a single pill. Whole pill is the target. Press it in the Warm cache state to see the only honest answer to "nothing came back".

B - if the header keeps a control, it should be one thing

The complaint reads as much like "those two do not belong together" as "that icon is wrong". T draws a sentence and then a separate circular button that repeats what the sentence is about. B fuses them: a 36px pill, hairline border, surfaceSunken fill, radiusPill, glyph 16 then the age at 13/600. The glyph stops being a button and becomes the pill's own leading mark.

The word "Updated" leaves

The pill carries the refresh mark, so the label does not have to say what it is about. That drops the trailing from about 145px to about 92px and removes the one piece of prose in a header row.

Two costs, stated
  • 36px is under the 44px target the rest of this app uses. It needs a transparent inset to reach 44 without drawing 44, which is a real but ordinary Flutter pattern.
  • Light mode needs care. textSecondary #5A606E on light surfaceSunken #CFD4DB is 4.23:1 - under AA. The pill's label must be textPrimary (12.47:1 light, 20.0:1 dark) and only the glyph may stay secondary.
Runner-up. The best answer if the header should keep a tap target - it is the only scheme where pressing refresh can say "nothing came back" in words. It loses to A on the plainer test: the phone did not need a second refresh at all.
C - freshness belongs to the list  rejected

Header is bare; the stamp joins the panel kicker. Now switch this phone to Cold start and watch the freshness disappear from the page entirely.

C - the tidiest header and the worst behaviour

The stamp moves into the digest panel's kicker line, taking the slot NEWEST FIRST occupies today - and that slot is offered by the code itself: "it is also the one piece of chrome on this panel that could go without changing what the page does" (crypto_news_screen.dart:626-628). The header becomes GWPageHeader(title: 'Crypto News'), byte-identical to Transactions, Markets and Assets.

Those are the three states where "how fresh is this?" is the actual question, and they are the three where this scheme has no answer. The shipped stamp is unconditional for exactly this reason (crypto_news_screen.dart:277-280).

And it collides with the ages it sits above

Every row below the kicker already prints a green age from the same formatter (_MetaLine, crypto_news_screen.dart:904-926). "UPDATED 3H AGO" one line above 29 of those is a different fact - when the FEED was fetched, not when the STORY was published - in the same words. The header never had that problem, because nothing near the page title is a story.

Rejected. It buys the cleanest header in the set and pays with the answer being absent in three states and ambiguous in the fourth. Kept in the sketch because the header it produces is worth looking at: that is what A's header would be if the stamp went too.
D - the other icon on this page  second reading

Header left exactly as shipped (the stamp still lies on a warm press - press it). What changes is _NewsPhoto.errorWidget. Switch the photos to Hero fails and All fail.

D - in case "that icon" meant the broken photo

The only other glyph a reader meets on Crypto News is Icons.broken_image_outlined on surfaceSunken (crypto_news_screen.dart:945-949). It is drawn at Flutter's default 24px in textSecondary, at every size the photo comes in, because errorWidget does not know how big it is.

Two problems, one of them semantic

A torn photograph is the wrong word. broken_image says something is broken. Nothing is: CoinTelegraph did not ship an image for that item, or the CDN 404ed, and the row still carries a headline, a dek and an age. The reader can neither fix it nor act on it.

And it is loudest where it matters least. A 24px mark inside 72px is 11% of the tile; the same mark inside the hero is 0.7%. The scale is backwards.

D's answer: size-aware and quiet
  • Under about 96px there is no glyph at all - the thumbnail is a plain surfaceSunken square. The row loses nothing: headline, dek and age are all still there.
  • At hero and tile size, Icons.image_not_supported_outlined at 20px in textSecondary (6.20:1 on surfaceSunken dark). It states an absence instead of a fault.
  • Optionally the source mark under it, in the kicker's own 9/700 uppercase, so the plate carries one fact.

The three candidate glyphs are drawn at real size below the phone so the difference is visible rather than argued.

Orthogonal, and cheap either way. This is a size test and a glyph swap inside one widget. If the reading of the request was wrong, this is the answer; if it was right, this is still worth doing and does not conflict with A, B or C.
