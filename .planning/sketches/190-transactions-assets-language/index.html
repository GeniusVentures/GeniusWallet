The one piece Transactions has and Assets does not

A filter track. Assets' panel 2 leads with a search field; Transactions' leads with five 44px chips (Filters.primary + the overflow trigger). That single difference is what the three schemes disagree about, and it is the whole sketch: where does a control go in a language whose panel 2 was designed around a text field?

Read row 4 before judging scheme B. Assets' panel 1 exists because a portfolio has ONE number that is the answer to "how am I doing". Whether an activity feed has an equivalent is not obvious, and B is the scheme that tries to find one.

What is wrong with it, precisely

Very little, and that is worth saying first - this page was designed, not defaulted. It has a real reason for its shape: the comment at :449-461 says the track gets its own row so its 44px targets clear the card wall, and the split is deliberate - the track is a control, the card is content.

The count is the real gap

Assets always tells you how many things it is showing and whether a filter is narrowing them ("3 of 11 assets", :440-442). Transactions tells you nothing: the footer count was deleted in Phase 12, and 023 gave the number to the wide rail's All row - which the phone does not render. So on a phone, filtering to Mint and seeing four rows leaves you unable to tell four-of-four from four-of-forty without clearing the filter.

That is a defect the boxing question does not touch, and every scheme below fixes it the same way, with the component Assets already uses. It is called out here so it is not mistaken for a benefit of any one scheme.
A - two panels

The track gets a box of its own. Panel, gap, panel - the rhythm OneColumnDashBoardView draws on Home and Assets now draws on a page.

A - Assets' two-panel shape, taken literally

Assets has a small panel over a big one. A copies that geometry exactly: the control lives in panel 1, the content in panel 2, space3 between them. The track's left edge moves from 6 to 14, landing on the row wall below it, so the page finally has one left edge instead of two.

The objection, which is real

Sketch 184 rejected boxed menu sections as a box over one row, and panel 1 here holds exactly one control. Assets' panel 1 is not analogous: it holds a kicker AND a 24px number, so it is a block of information. A's panel 1 is a container around a pill.

The mitigation in the sketch: the panel is full width where the track is intrinsic, so it does one thing the track cannot - it gives the control the row wall as a left edge. Whether that is worth 14px of vertical and a hairline is exactly what you are looking at.

A is the conservative answer. It changes geometry and nothing else: no new number, no new claim, no question about what a summary would say. If B's band does not earn its place, A is where this lands.
B - the Assets twin

Panel 1 carries a number, like Assets does. Two versions of that number are toggleable under the phone, because they are not equally honest.

B - what would Transactions' "total value" be?

This is the scheme you asked to see both ways, and building it turned up a hard constraint that decides it. Toggle NUMBER between the two candidates:

b1 - counts (the honest one)

"24 transactions, 30 days" with the sent/received split beneath. Every term is a length over a list the page already holds, computed by filterCounts() which already exists and already runs on this screen (transactions_slim_view.dart:129). Nothing can be missing, nothing can be stale, and it is true for a wallet with no price feed at all.

b2 - net fiat flow (the one that reads better and lies)

"+ $1,842.19 in 30 days" in numericHeadline, the same type as Assets' total. It is the more satisfying number and it cannot be computed correctly. The value line on a transaction row falls back to No price when the token has no market data (transaction_displays.dart:366) - the sketch's BTC row shows it. A net-flow total must either silently drop those rows or print a total that is not the sum of what is on screen.

Turn NUMBER to b2 and then set data to BUSY. The band prints a total, and one row below it says No price. That is a number claiming to summarise a list it is provably not summarising. The footnote under it ("excludes 1 unpriced") is the mitigation, and a summary that needs a footnote explaining what it left out is a summary that has not earned its panel.
The deeper problem, and it is not about honesty

Assets' total answers a question the user actually has - what is my wallet worth - and it is the same number on the dashboard, which is why 7b2fd474 promoted AssetsTotalBand to a shared widget so the two could not drift. There is no equivalent question for an activity feed. Neither b1 nor b2 is a number anyone opens this tab to read, and both cost ~92px of the 628px viewport - a quarter of the first screen - to say it.

Recommendation: reject B, and keep b1's count. Not the band - the count. It belongs in panel 2's kicker where Assets puts it, where it costs 16px instead of 92 and sits next to the thing it counts. B's real contribution is proving that the number Transactions was missing is a kicker, not a hero.
C - one panel  recommended

Assets' panel 2, and nothing else. Track, kicker with the count, rows - one box, the same order and the same walls Assets uses.

What actually makes Assets read the way it does is the inside of panel 2: a control at the top, a kicker that counts what is below it, then rows at a shared wall. What the track does at full card width

Today the track is intrinsic and left-aligned on the canvas. Inside the panel it keeps its intrinsic width - a pill stretched to 348px would be a different control - but its left edge is the card wall, which is the row wall, which is the kicker's wall. One edge for everything on the page. Set RULER on and compare the vertical dashed line down A, B and C.

The count, which is the actual fix

"4 OF 24 TRANSACTIONS" appears the moment a filter is active - the exact string shape assets_screen.dart:440 builds. Tap Mint with data on BUSY and read it. This is the defect named in the control tab, fixed with a component that is already on this screen's sibling and costs 16px.

Cost, stated

Against today: +20px of vertical (the panel's 6px top padding, the hairline, and space6 replacing the current space6 below the track, plus the 16px kicker) and one space3 gap removed by not having a second panel. Against A: -14px and one fewer container. Row content width is 348 in all three - the boxing already happened.

Recommended. It is the only scheme where every piece is a piece Assets already uses, in the order Assets uses it; it adds the count without adding a panel; and it is the smallest diff of the three - the existing DashboardScrollContainer at :483 simply grows two children above the list.
The one thing to check on the phone, not here. The track inside the panel sits 6px nearer the card's rounded corner than it does on the canvas. At radiusLg 15 with a 44px chip that measured clean in this sketch, but corner clearance is the sort of thing that reads differently on glass - it is the first thing to look at when this is running.
