2 · Amount · Value · Status for the rows where money did not simply move

You called the current rendering misleading. It is, and the code says why (transaction_utils.dart:356-366): both a failed transaction and a processing job are given amount = '—', and the real number is demoted to the small grey line underneath.

Computing is not a real choice — you are simply right. A job spends tx.fees; that GNUS genuinely leaves the wallet. Printing a dash in the loudest slot and 0.42 GNUS spent in the quietest inverts the hierarchy. All three schemes below fix it identically: − 0.42 GNUS in Amount, $0.32 fee in Value, status Complete.

The failed row is the real decision — and here I want to push back on one half of your instruction. If a failed send prints − 0.75 ETH in the same slot, same weight, same colour as a successful one, the row states that 0.75 ETH left the wallet. It did not. Anyone scanning or mentally summing the column double-counts money that never moved. So: show the amount — you are right that a dash tells the user nothing — but do not let it claim the balance changed. Three ways to draw that line. Switch them at the top.

Why c. It is one rule — centre inside the slot, but never search more than 480px for the middle — instead of a hand-picked offset. In a short panel it behaves exactly as today, so nothing regresses where the current layout is already fine; in your 1400px panel it stops at 240px from the top instead of drifting to 700.
