The size question, which is not a size question

Jakub, 2026-07-28: "te ikony powinny być zaraz przy tytule i wysokości tytułu, wydaje mi się, nie większe." He is right about the look: headlineLg is 24/32, so a 44px chip stands 12px taller than the line it sits on and the header reads as if the buttons are the headline.

But 44 is not an arbitrary number here, and shrinking it is not free. GWButton's sm size carries its own history in the source: return 44; // was 36 — touch floor (iOS 44). Somebody already lowered it and reversed the decision. WCAG 2.5.5 (AAA) asks 44×44; 2.5.8 (AA) asks 24×24. So 32 clears AA and drops the app below the AAA bar it currently meets - and this page renders on a phone below 768.

The resolution is that the painted box and the touch target do not have to be the same thing. Use the Icon size switch above:

OptionWhat you seeTouch targetCode cost
44 chip (shipped)a 44px circle, 12px taller than the title44 ✓ AAAnone - this is today
32 = title heighta circle exactly the title's line box32 - drops to AA unless GWButton gains a target-vs-paint parameter, which is one new param for one consumer1 param, or an accepted regression
bare glyph ★a 20px glyph on the title's baseline, no box at all, the circle appearing only on hover44 ✓ AAA - the box is still there, it is just transparentnone - it is GWButton.icon's own default variant

The third one is the answer to what was actually asked. "Not bigger than the title" is about what you SEE, and with the fill and hairline gone there is nothing to be bigger - the glyph is 20px against a 32px line. The 44px hit area survives untouched because it was never visible in the first place. And it needs no code: GWButton.icon already defaults to ghost; today's page passes variant: GWButtonVariant.icon explicitly to get the chip.

The cost, stated: at rest the icons have no boundary, so they read as glyphs until you hover. That is acceptable under 1.4.11 - a control's edge must clear 3:1 only when the edge is what identifies the control, and here the glyph is, at 15.8:1 dark and 16.1:1 light. It is the same argument GWSelectRow recorded for its check mark.

What to look for

Switch "Data" to "No market data" and walk all six. That is the route in from the wallet's own Assets list for any coin CoinGecko does not cover, and it is where two of these six fall over - C and D both hang off the stat rail, which does not exist on that page. The panel says so in red rather than quietly re-homing them.

Then set "Route in" to Markets, which is the main way in. There the live count is two, because Bridge is GNUS-only. A placement that looks balanced with three icons and lopsided with two is not a placement, it is a picture of one state.

Recommendation, revised after Jakub's note on size

★ E2 · Beside the identity, divided, as bare glyphs. Jakub's constraint decides it: actions right at the title, no taller than it. E2 is the only combination that delivers that literally - the glyph is 20px against a 32px line, so nothing in the row is bigger than the headline - while keeping the 44px target the repo already reversed one decision to protect. It costs zero new height, it is pixel-identical on every route and in every state because it never touches market data, and the hairline answers the one real objection to plain E: without it the icons read as part of the name.

Runner-up: A · Beside the price - my previous pick, and it stays strong for one reason E2 cannot match: GWPageHeader.trailing is where this app puts actions, on every other page. E2 moves them to the left half, which is a convention change, not just a position. Take A if the walk shows the left header feeling crowded once the token logo, the name and three glyphs share it.

Rejected: D · Seventh rail tile (unchanged, see below), and now also 32px chips as a way to hit title height: they clear WCAG 2.5.8 AA but drop the app below the 2.5.5 AAA bar it currently meets, and buying that costs either a new GWButton parameter for one consumer or an accepted regression on a page that also renders on a phone. The bare glyph gets the same look for neither price.

The earlier recommendation, kept for the record

A · Beside the price. It is where Jakub pointed - "obok ceny po prawej stronie u góry" - and it is the only one of the six that is free in both directions: zero new height, and it survives the no-data page without moving, because the header's trailing slot simply holds the icons instead of the price. It also puts the actions next to the number they act on, which is the association the whole page is built around. Against F, which ships today, it hands back the ~44px the PRICE line spends on nothing else.

Runner-up: E · Beside the identity. The most robust of the six - it is the only placement that depends on no market data whatsoever, so it is pixel-identical on every route and in every state, and it is the best of the six at a single action, because one glyph after a name is an ordinary shape. Not the pick because actions beside a title read as being about the title, and because the left half of the header is where the eye lands first - which a Bridge button does not deserve.

Rejected: D · Seventh rail tile. It fits beautifully at three actions and costs nothing, which is exactly why it is worth naming as a trap. The rail is six facts; a seventh cell full of buttons says the buttons are a seventh fact. It also disappears entirely on the no-data page, and the rail re-wraps 6→3→2 by width, so the action cell would land in a different place at every breakpoint.

Unchanged by any of the six, and it still decides the count: isGnusWalletConnected is hardcoded false at markets_screen.dart:58 and dashboard_markets.dart:83, and never passed at markets_search_bar.dart:93. So from Markets the row is two icons. If that is an oversight rather than a decision, every panel here gains a third - and A, which is the tightest of the six on a narrow window, is the one that feels it.
Contrast, unchanged from 073 and re-checked here. Glyph textPrimary on the card fill: 15.8:1 dark / 16.1:1 light, against 3.0 needed. The button edge borderSubtle is 1.32:1 dark / 1.27:1 light, which is acceptable for the same reason GWSelectRow recorded: under 1.4.11 the boundary must clear 3:1 when the boundary is what identifies the control, and here the glyph is. Every icon carries tooltip + semanticLabel, or it announces nothing.
