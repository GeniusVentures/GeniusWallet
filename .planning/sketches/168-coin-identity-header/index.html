Recommendation: ★ D. It does everything you asked - the price sits with the name, the Receive/Bridge row is gone, the header drops from three rows to one - while leaving the page able to do the one thing a user on a coin page most plausibly came to do. It also settles, by deletion, the open question 07-09 flagged for this walk: with Receive and Bridge gone there is no outline pair left to adjudicate against the one fill.

Runner-up: A. Cleanest header of the five and the literal reading of your annotation. Choose it if you want the coin page to be a quote sheet, and say so out loud - it is a product decision, not a layout tweak.

Rejected: B. Sketch 165 already decided this page uses labels, not bare glyphs, and change 3 exists specifically because icon-only actions on the title line were the problem. Making them 44px instead of 32px answers the accessibility half of that objection and none of the comprehension half.

C is not wrong, it is a different question. It is the only scheme where the price keeps full weight without sharing a baseline with the title - worth keeping in the drawer if A or D ever read as cramped on a long coin name.
