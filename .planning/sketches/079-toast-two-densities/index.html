Why two densities, not one

The 26 toast calls split 14 success · 13 warning · 3 error, and the 32 SnackBars are mostly "Link copied", "Checkout link copied", "Order ${status}". Those are two different jobs:

  • Ambient — the user just tapped copy. They know. A bordered card with a bold title, a 36px icon and a close button is shouting a receipt at them. Compact, ~2s, no close.
  • Alert — something failed and the user did not ask for it. Card, dismissible, longer, announced assertively.

One widget, one showToast call, a ToastDensity enum. Not two systems again — that is the mistake being undone.

Mobile specifics this settles

  • Offset is derived, never guessed: padding.top + kHeaderHeight + space4 = 44 + 60 + 8 = 112 on a 14 Pro, and correct by construction on every other device.
  • Swipe up to dismiss is the primary affordance, with a grab handle to advertise it. The top of a phone is out of thumb reach — a 44pt target there is still a stretch, so the gesture matters more than the button.
  • Duration scales with length — ~2s compact, 5s card, and never auto-dismiss while a screen reader is announcing.
  • Reduced motion — the 300ms slide is currently unconditional. Cross-fade when the OS asks for it.
  • Bottom was considered and rejected: kMobileBarHeight 60 plus the dock overhang plus the bottom inset means a bottom toast either covers the nav or floats ~90px up, which reads as detached. Top also matches where iOS puts system banners, which suits a wallet's alerts.

Making it used everywhere — the cheap part

showAppSnackBar is 18 lines and fans out to 26 call sites. Point its body at ToastManager and all 26 convert in one edit, with no call-site churn. Then 6 direct showSnackBar calls by hand.

Two findings that shrink it further: zero callers pass an action — the parameter is dead, so the toast needs no action slot; and 4 pass backgroundColor, hand-rolling what ToastType already means.

Colours are not being redesigned

Every value here is the shipping token, both modes, from the 23-03 contrast work: success #0AD89C dark / #07875F light (4.5:1 on white), error #FF4D4D / #D92D2D (4.8:1), surface #0C0E14 / #FFFFFF, secondary text #8A8F9D / #5A606E. The accent moves from a 2px full ring to a 4px leading edge — same colour, less ink. Title and message stay on the neutral ladder, so the toast still never depends on a status colour for legibility.

Open question for the walk

The compact pill is centred here and the card is full-width inset. That is deliberate — an auto-width pill reads as ambient, a full-width card as an alert — but it means two toasts of different densities on one stack do not share a left edge. Variant C shows exactly that. Worth a look on the device before it is built.
