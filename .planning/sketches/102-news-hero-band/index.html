102 · The hero band — B2 kept, then improved twice

What "Next up" is: the hero shows one lead story big, and beside it a short ranked list of the next few headlines — numbered, text only, no photo. Like a newspaper front page: one splash, then "also today". It buys density above the fold without giving every story a full photo card. Its weakness in plain B2 is that it only fixes the top row — items 5-30 still scroll as full cards. Both new variants keep B2's band and fix that tail.

B · Magazine

parent · for reference

The winner of sketch 100: one hero, then an even 3-column grid of full photo cards for everything else. No "Next up". The starting point both improvements build on.

B2 · Hero + Next up

from sketch 101 · for reference

Hero shares the first row with a Next up column (items 2-4, headline only). Great first impression — but items 5-30 are still full-size photo cards, so the page stays ~7 screens long. The two variants to its right each attack that.

B2-I · Front page

recommendedreuses GWSectionTitle

B2's band, then straight to the list. Hero + Next up (items 2-5) is the whole photo treatment; below it a single GWSectionTitle('Earlier') and every remaining story as a compact 88×60 row. One splash, four ranked also-reads, then a scannable ledger. ~3 screens, and only one story wears a big photo — which is the honest number of stories that deserve one. Fewest photo cards to lay out of any variant.

B2-II · Featured trio

reuses GWSectionTitle

Three stories get a photo, not one. A tall hero on the left; on the right two medium photo sub-cards stacked (items 2-3). Under the band, Next up carries items 4-7 as headline rows, then GWSectionTitle('Earlier') and the rest as compact tail rows. Richer, more magazine-like top; costs one extra photo layout and a touch more height than I. ~4 screens. Pick this if B2-I feels too text-heavy below the hero.

Everything still maps to shipped components

Same map as sketch 101 — the only net-new widget is the compact tail row (~40 lines), shared by I and II. The one real gap remains: GWCard has no hover state (gw_card.dart:62-71 wraps onTap in an InkWell, not the 008 D lift). Add a hoverLift flag once and every card here — hero, photo card, sub-card — gets the standard hover. "Next up" and tail rows are plain InkWell list rows, which is exactly what an InkWell is for, so those need nothing new.

What to look for

1. Switch to 900 and scroll each to the bottom — that is the page-length comparison. 2. Compare the top row of I vs II: is one big photo enough (I), or do you want three (II)? 3. Hover the Next up rows and the tail rows — those are the plain list-row hovers, distinct from the card lift. 4. Switch to 420: the Next up column drops below the hero, sub-cards stack, tail rows stay legible at 88×60. 5. Type in search — everything re-ranks live.
