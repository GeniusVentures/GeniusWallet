071 · The coin page, the whole screen

Jakub: “zrób redesign tego całego screenu, łącznie z navigation barem u góry” — “bo wygląda słabo”. It does, and the three reasons are measurable rather than matters of taste. Every panel below is the page at its real width, with the app's real navbar, and its content composed from components that already exist. The card layer inside it is 070-A, shipped today.

The three reasons it reads weak

1 · SizedBox(height: 480). One literal, and it is most of the problem. _buildMainCard hard-codes 480px. The Info + Convert column beside it comes out around 530. So on a 1335px-tall window the page's content ends at roughly 620px and the remaining half is empty — that is the void drawn in red on variant 0. Nothing is broken; the layout simply never asked for the room it has.
2 · This is the only screen in the app without a navbar. GoRoute('/token-info') sits outside the ShellRoute (router.dart:243, the shell closes at 242). Every tab — Dashboard, Swap, Markets, News, Settings — is inside it. So from a coin you cannot reach News without going back first, and the screen you came from is still one click behind you rather than on screen.
3 · The frame is a different frame. maxWidth: 1200, centred, with space10 padding. Markets, Transactions and News all use xxl (1536) with a 12px gutter, so their title sits at x = 12. Here it lands ~170px further in, on a 1500px window. Same app, two page frames — and you cross between them with one click.
4 · The model carries 24 fields. The page shows 6. CoinGeckoMarketData already holds marketCapRank, high24h, low24h, fullyDilutedValuation, ath + athChangePercentage + athDate, atl, maxSupply, priceChange24h, marketCapChangePercentage24h and lastUpdated — all in Hive, all fetched already. Filling the void costs zero new requests. That is what variant B spends.

Where every element comes from

What to look at

1 · Switch 0 → A and watch the top of the window. That is the biggest change on this page and it is invisible in a screenshot of the content.

2 · Look at the bottom of variant 0. The red hatch is real empty page, drawn to scale at a 1335px window height.

3 · Drop to 900. Below 1024 the layout already stacks; check your favourite survives it, because 152-D froze the <768 layout and it still has to arrive there.

4 · Send and Swap are grey in every variant, because they are genuinely disabled (D-01/D-02) and More is GNUS-gated. A variant that only looks good with a full row of live buttons would be lying.

Recommendation

★ B · Stat rail. A fixes the structure but leaves a taller chart in a page that is still thin; B fixes the structure and the emptiness, using data already sitting in Hive. The KPI tile is the only new shape, and it is `GWCard` + `GWKicker` + a value — the Markets hero already reads this way, which is the page you arrive from.

Runner-up: A · In the frame. The honest minimum and genuinely a big improvement on its own: navbar, one frame, a chart that uses the window. Take this one if the page should be repaired rather than re-composed — it is also strictly a subset of B, so it is never wasted work.

Rejected: C · Your position. It reads well and it answers the wrong question. This page is reached from Markets, where you are looking at what something is worth, not at what you hold — the dashboard already answers that, and Receive/Send/Swap are one row away here. C also degrades to nothing for a coin you do not own, which on Markets is most of them.
