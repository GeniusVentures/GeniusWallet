068 · One list-picker, three drawers

Design question: Select Network (network_dropdown_selector.dart:93), SDK Accounts (sdk_account_manager.dart:72) and Your Accounts (account_dropdown_selector.dart:164) all do one job — pick one item from a list — and ship three different rows, two different headers and two different footers. None has been looked at since the shell changed. What does one archetype look like on the canvas the drawers took today (156-A panel, surfaceSunken controls, GWKicker, GWDetailGrid) — and where does it stop being one, given two of the three have footers and one has a per-row overflow menu?

The one that is not in the shell at all. SDK Accounts passes no title to ResponsiveDrawer.show — deliberately, so the shell does not draw a competing AppBar — and renders BottomDrawer inside instead. That was a stand-off before today. After 156-A it is a visible defect: the panel is surfaceElevated #0C0E14 and BottomDrawer paints itself surfaceMenu #171A21, so the header is now a lighter block sitting inside its own drawer. Every variant here deletes that call and gives the drawer the shell's header, which is also how it gets the ✕ back on the right where the other eighteen have it.
Sketch 066-C said "no footer — a pick is the action". That is wrong for two of the three. Your Accounts carries Add Wallet and SDK Accounts carries Add with mnemonic + Add with private key. The archetype needs a footer slot that is allowed to be empty, not a rule that there is no footer.

Why every variant keeps the check glyph, and why that is not decoration. Selection here is a state, and 1.4.11 covers states. The tint measures 1.39:1 and the brand edge 1.60:1 — neither can carry it alone, which is exactly the finding sketch 156 made about a field's fill. The glyph at brandPrimaryStrong measures 6.81:1 and carries it by itself. So: tint + edge for the eye, glyph for the requirement. _TokenRow already ships all three; the other two drawers ship none of them.
