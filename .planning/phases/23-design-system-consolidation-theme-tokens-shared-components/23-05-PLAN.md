<objective>
Collapse the one duplicated pattern in this codebase that can be collapsed **provably** without a
visual baseline, and write down honestly why the other candidates could not.

Twelve sites hand-roll the identical hover pattern: a boolean field, a pointer region with enter and
exit callbacks that `setState` it, and a child that reacts. `lib/squid_router/swap_field.dart` already
contains the correct extraction of it as a private shim, whose own doc comment says it is "a local
shim, not a component, and it disappears the day these two controls become buttons." Promote it.

Two rules govern and neither is negotiable. **Rule of Three** — two occurrences do not justify a
shared component, three do; duplication is cheaper than the wrong abstraction, and the Rule of Three
is a floor, not a quota. **Widgets, not helper methods** — a real widget class, never a `_buildFoo()`
returning a `Widget`, because a helper rebuilds the whole enclosing widget, cannot be `const`, and is
invisible to the DevTools inspector.

Purpose: one hover implementation, and an honest record of the extractions this phase declined.
Output: `GWHoverable`, its test, twelve migrated sites, and the extraction audit.
</objective>
