  <name>Task 1: One navigation rule for every platform</name>
  <files>lib/gnus_bridge/gnus_dashboard_origins.dart, test/gnus_bridge/gnus_dashboard_origins_test.dart</files>
  <behavior>
    - The frame kind is one of main, sub, unknown (Linux), or newWindow.
    - main: an allowlisted origin is allowed; an off-list http(s) URL opens externally; any other scheme is blocked.
    - unknown: an allowlisted origin, `about:blank` or `about:srcdoc` is allowed; everything else is blocked and nothing opens.
    - sub: allowed. The frame cannot reach the bridge, so it needs no lock.
    - newWindow: never allowed in the view; http(s) opens externally, anything else is blocked.
    - Origins compare as `GnusOrigin` values, so `HTTPS://Host:443/x` matches `https://host`.
  </behavior>
  <action>Add a pure `gnusNavigation({required Uri? url, required GnusFrame frame, required Set<GnusOrigin> allowed})` returning `allow`, `block` or `external`, per D-13. Give it a table-driven test, one row per behavior.</action>
