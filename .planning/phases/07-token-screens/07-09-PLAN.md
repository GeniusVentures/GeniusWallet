**Re-derive the `perRow` ladder (`:1264-1268`).** This is the part that is easy to miss and it will be

candidate is `5 / 3 / 2`, and its 3+2 and 2+2+1 tails are exactly the case the test has to judge; if
they leave holes, the ladder is wrong and the honest fix is a ladder whose steps divide five. This is
a layout decision to be made against a measurement, not guessed - which is why it is a behaviour
