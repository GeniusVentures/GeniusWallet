  <name>Task 1: Forward eventsFor(origin) to each gnus session, in order</name>
  <files>lib/reown/gnus_wallet_connect.dart, test/reown/gnus_wallet_connect_lifecycle_test.dart</files>
  <behavior>
    - Session approved with all three gnus events and connected in the store: a harness wallet switch emits `gnus_accountsChanged` once on `eip155:80002` with the `gnus_getAccounts` payload; a network switch emits `gnus_networkChanged`
    - A session that approved only `gnus_accountsChanged` gets no `gnus_networkChanged` emit call at all
    - Origin not connected, or revoked: no emits; a later connect resumes without resubscribing
    - `gnus_jobUpdated` reaches only the session whose origin submitted the job
    - A session present in `getActiveSessions` at attach time forwards; firing `onSessionDelete` or `onSessionExpire` for its topic stops it; the disposer stops all
    - An emit that throws is logged by type, and the next event still goes out
    - Ordering: an event emitted while a reply is pending on the same topic, and queued first, goes out first
  </behavior>
  <action>Per D-09, keep a map from topic to subscription inside `handleGnusRequests`. Subscribe a topic when either
    holds: (a) its session's eip155 namespace has a gnus event and its origin parses (same rule as requests),
    for every session in `getActiveSessions()` at attach time; or (b) it arrives through `onSessionConnect`.

    Forward each `handler.eventsFor(origin)` event as `emitSessionEvent` on `eip155:80002`, as
    `SessionEventParams(name, data: payload)`. Skip names the session did not approve; sign-client would drop
    them anyway, and Reown throws on them.

    Cancel on `onSessionDelete`, on `onSessionExpire`, and in the disposer.

    Route replies and emits for one topic through one per-topic future chain, so they leave in the order they were
    queued. A failure inside the chain is caught and logged by type, and never breaks the chain.

    Per D-10, emit no `disconnect`: the session's end is the page's signal.</action>
