- `GnusEvent(name, payload)`: `name` a `GnusRpcEventName` value, `payload` a `Map<String, Object?>`
- `({String role, String? main}) gnusNodeRole(String node, Map<String, Iterable<String>>? registrations)`: pure, lowercased compare. Child of M when another key M lists the node; `main` when the node's own list is non-empty; else `none`; null registrations -> `none`. The child spend guard stays the gate (`checking` blocks), not this function.

Result shapes are the contract's section 3. `gnus_getEarnings`: `{sdkAddress, balance, processing: {isProcessing, jobsProcessed: null}, earned: {total: null, history: null}}`.
