<objective>
DAP-02, at exactly the confidence the evidence supports: Squid's calldata
decodes on its input side and nowhere else. The drawer says what is going in and
admits it cannot read what comes out.

Purpose: the last requirement, plus the phase gate. Output: a real
captured-calldata fixture, the router allow-list, and a measured close-out that
records the deviation instead of papering over it.

**Why input-only:** at fixed offsets in the recorded live response, word 0 is
exactly `fromToken.address` and word 1 is exactly `fromAmount`, but
`toToken.address` appears only nested at a route-dependent position and
`toAmount` does not appear at all. Finding the destination by scanning the blob
for a known token address is a heuristic a hostile payload can seed, and a
signing screen is the wrong place for one (D-11).

**AGENTS.md, non-negotiable:** brace every `if` with the body on its own line;
