Three small, independent corrections to the dashboard panel — and the one refactor 15-04 depends
on.

Purpose:
1. **The behavioural bug sketch 020 found.** The filter bar renders on a wallet with ZERO
   transactions: five controls offering to filter nothing, above a block that says there is
   nothing. The branch that knows this already exists at `:246` (`scoped.isEmpty`); it simply never
   reaches the header at `:192`. Locked in sketch 021: hidden entirely, page rail *and* panel
   chips.
2. **The empty-state icon.** `Icons.receipt_long_outlined` -> `Icons.sync_alt`, locked in sketch
   022.
3. **`_activeLabelShader` is trapped inside `_TransactionFilterBar`** (`:490`). 15-04's rail
   underline must be painted through that exact function, because the brand blue stop
   **`#0AAEE6`** (`GeniusWalletColors.gradientBlue`, `genius_wallet_colors.dart:85-86`) measures
   **2.56:1** on white and fails WCAG 1.4.11's 3:1 for a non-text UI mark — the shader is where the
   light-mode degradation to `brandPrimaryOnSurface` (`#0A6885`, 6.30:1 on white) lives. Hoisting
   it to file scope is expected and is the whole reason this plan runs before 15-04.
   **`#14C8FF` does not exist in this codebase** — it appeared in an earlier draft of the design
   notes and in sketch 022's contrast table, and it is being corrected there separately. Do not
   write it into any comment, test or summary; the conclusion is unchanged (2.56 < 3.0 fails,
   6.30 passes) but the number must be the real one.

This plan deliberately does NOT touch the chips themselves. The dashboard panel keeps its existing
chips + `⋯` menu, unchanged — the rail is page-only (sketch 020's named risk, accepted).

Output: three edits to `transactions_slim_view.dart`, and the test-fixture repair those edits
force.
