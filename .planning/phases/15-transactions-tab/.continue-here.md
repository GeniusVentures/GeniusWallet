## Three things the plans got wrong, already corrected in place

Recorded so nobody re-derives them:

1. **A test on a hand-copied replica of the screen's tree could not fail.** Mutating `xl → xxl` ran
   **green** on the replica and **red** on the real screen. `15-05`'s test now pumps the real
   `TransactionsScreen`.
2. **The width arithmetic was inverted.** With `Padding` outside the `ConstrainedBox` the cap binds
   the content and the gutter is additive, so the answer is **1280**, not 1280 − 24. Writing what the
   plan said would have failed against correct code.
3. **The `DashboardScrollContainer` content box is 194, not 196** — `GWDecorations.surface` carries a
   1px hairline border on top of its padding.
