<task type="checkpoint:decision" gate="blocking">
  <decision>What a non-success terminal status is recorded as</decision>
  <context>`TransactionStatus` has four values: pending, cancelled, completed, failed. Squid reports
  seven, including PARTIAL_SUCCESS, NEEDS_GAS and REFUNDED. Recording a partial swap as completed
  overstates it; as failed understates it. This decides what a user is told happened to their money.</context>
  <options>
    <option id="map-to-pending"><name>Partial, needs-gas and not-found → pending; refunded → cancelled; failed-destination → failed</name>
      <pros>No storage migration, and nothing is claimed that did not happen.</pros>
      <cons>A partial swap sits pending indefinitely with no affordance to finish it.</cons></option>
    <option id="extend-enum"><name>Add a partial value to the Hive-backed TransactionStatus enum</name>
      <pros>The row says what actually happened, and the receipt can too.</pros>
      <cons>A Hive enum change across every stored transaction, plus receipt and list rendering — a phase of its own.</cons></option>
  </options>
  <resume-signal>Select: map-to-pending or extend-enum</resume-signal>
</task>

  <behavior>
    - Route re-fetch throws, or transactionRequest is absent or unparseable → no hash, nothing sent.
    - Allowance sufficient → no approval call, and the swap proceeds.
    - Allowance short → one approval for exactly the swap amount and for no other amount.
    - Approval fails or is rejected → no hash, and no send was attempted.
    - Send fails → no hash. Send succeeds → the hash, and only this shape carries one.
    - For every outcome with no hash, the side-effect triple is all false.
  </behavior>
  <action>
  Create `lib/squid_router/swap_execution.dart` as a pure module — no Flutter import, no
  `BuildContext`, every dependency injected as a function parameter (route fetch, allowance read,
  approve, send, status poll), the shape of `swap_cta_state.dart`. Sequence: re-fetch the route with
  `quoteOnly` false → unwrap `transactionRequest` into the hex map `signAndSendTransaction` expects,
  mapping `target` to `to` and supplying the wallet address as `from` → `decideApproval` → approve if
  needed → send → return the outcome.
  Define `SwapOutcome` with exactly one shape carrying a hash, plus a top-level
  `sideEffectsFor(SwapOutcome)` returning three booleans: show toast, show receipt, store row. All
  three are false for every shape without a hash; the screen may not decide this for itself.
  Record a non-quoteOnly `/v2/route` body as `route_response_executable.json`, address redacted to
  all-zero, and test the unwrap against it — 26-03's quoteOnly fixture has no transactionRequest and
  cannot prove this. Keep the key out of this module: it takes a send callback, never a private key.
