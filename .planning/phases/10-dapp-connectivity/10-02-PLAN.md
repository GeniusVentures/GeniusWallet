Measured at planning (reown_walletkit 1.4.0; 1.5.1's init() is identical):
- `ReownWalletKit.init()` sets `_initialized` only after every step succeeds, and each sub-init has its own guard, so a retry on the same client is safe up to the Pay step.
- `pay` is a `late final` assigned inside init(). After a failed pay.init(), every retry throws LateInitializationError, and the restart toast (D-05) covers that. Plan 01's desktop stub means pay.init cannot fail on desktop.
- `getActiveSessions()` throws until init has run (sign_engine.dart:703). After a failed startup, `_initializeWalletKit` never reaches `_attachWalletKitListeners()`. A retry that fixed init alone would pair, and the approve drawer would never appear.
- `RelayClient._connect` swallows connection errors, so being offline may not make init fail at all. Do not assume that it does.
