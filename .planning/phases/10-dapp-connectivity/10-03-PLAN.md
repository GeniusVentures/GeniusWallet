- `class PairDappDrawer` with `static Future<void> show({required BuildContext context, required String wcUri, required bool startWithPaste, required Future<bool> Function(Uri uri) onPair})`. It calls `ResponsiveDrawer.show<void>` with title 'WalletConnect', a body and a footer.

- Pop with the footer's own context, which lives inside the drawer route. A caller's context pops the wrong navigator, as the swap settings comment records.
- Colours come from `context.gw` or the GW components only.
