**1f. `RetryProcessingStatus` on repeated failure: no backoff. Settled, with evidence.**

Sketch 166 leaves this open. It is answered by reading the code, and the answer is that the failure
mode a backoff would protect against does not exist:

  A throw stops the poll immediately.
- `_onRetryProcessingStatus` (`app_bloc.dart:314-324`) only calls `_startProcessingPolling()` and
  emits `neverTicked`. `_startProcessingPolling` cancels before it re-creates

  aggressive one possible.

`_onRetryProcessingStatus` recording this decision and its reasoning, and record it in the SUMMARY.
