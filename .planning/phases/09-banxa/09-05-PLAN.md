<objective>
Re-skin the two surfaces the checkout hand-off passes through: the QR screen, and the checkout
options sheet that every buy and order flow opens.

`checkout_qr.dart` is nearly literal-free already — its re-skin is chrome, typography and one
button swap. `handle_banxa_drawer.dart` is the opposite: it is entirely unthemed Material
defaults, it is invoked from 4 call sites across 3 in-scope files, and 09-CONTEXT D-07 brought it
into scope precisely so that no re-skinned Banxa screen opens a pre-redesign sheet — a seam a walk
would catch immediately.

Purpose: close the visible seam between the re-skinned screens and the sheet they all hand off to,
without changing where that sheet appears or what it does.
Output: both files re-skinned in place, two widget tests, and a filed todo recording that the
sheet's visual contract was derived rather than specified.
</objective>
