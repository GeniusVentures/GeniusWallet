<objective>
Re-skin `/markets` to sketch 103 **H1 "Refined split"**: a native-token (GENIUS AI) hero card
over a sortable "All Markets" table, replacing the old flat market card grid.

Purpose: the Markets tab was the last of the primary content tabs still on the pre-redesign flat
grid. Sketch 103 explored the page and picked winner **H1** — pull the house token out of the list
and give it a proper hero (identity, oversized price, change pill, a 2×2 stat block, and a 7d
brand-gradient chart), then present everything else as a CoinGecko/CMC-style sortable table beneath
it. The data path does not change: the page already made two chained fetches (coins, then market
data), and this phase keeps both, each with its own error/retry, and reads every displayed field
off the existing `CoinGeckoMarketData`. No new API, model, or Hive box.
