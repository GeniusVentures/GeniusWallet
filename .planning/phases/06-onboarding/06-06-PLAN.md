<objective>
Close Phase 6 by proving, in one place, the two things no individual screen plan can prove: that the
UI-SPEC §3 security gate still holds across the FINISHED tree, and that both onboarding flows actually
complete end to end on a genuinely fresh install. Then put every deliberately-unfixed gap on the record
as a filed todo, so the phase's honest scope is legible instead of implied.

Why this plan exists rather than being folded into 06-05. Two reasons, both from this project's own
recorded experience:
1. **The security gate is cross-file.** §3's six properties span `recovery_phrase_screen.dart`,
   `verify_recovery_phrase_screen.dart`, `paste_field.dart`, `pin_screen.dart` and the bloc files.
   Each of 06-03..06-05 checked its own slice against its own diff. Nothing has checked the SET, against
   the finished tree, after all edits landed. A property that held in every individual diff can still be
   false in the union.
2. **STATE's walk-protocol lesson.** "A walk driven by fixtures verifies the fixture's state, not the
   product's. Zero-states, error-states and first-run states need explicit walk steps or they are
   silently exempted from every review. Phase 05 was walked six times and shipped a fresh-install
   overflow." Onboarding IS the first-run flow. Each of 06-01..06-05 walked its own screen; none walked
   an uninterrupted run of the whole thing from a cleared data directory. That run is ROADMAP criterion
   1's actual wording — "complete end to end on a fresh install" — and it is the one thing most likely
   to be assumed.

This plan writes exactly one small script and no `lib/` code. If the gate or the walk fails, the fix
belongs in a gap-closure plan (06-07), not in a quiet amendment here.

Scope fences to re-confirm, not re-open (UI-SPEC §5): the 8 logic-only files (`new_pin_cubit.dart`,
`new_pin_state.dart`, the three `existing_wallet/bloc/` files, the three `new_wallet/bloc/` files) have
no UI surface and must be untouched; `backup_phrase_screen.dart` is verified-unreachable dead code and
must be untouched. §5.3's reconciliation — 8 logic-only + 1 dead + 13 table-carrying = 22 — is the
count this plan audits against the git diff.
