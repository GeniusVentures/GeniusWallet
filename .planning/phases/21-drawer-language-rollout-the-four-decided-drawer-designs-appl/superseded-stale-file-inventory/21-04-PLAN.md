<objective>
Bring the five result drawers onto 031-B1. These are the receipts with no row
data: an icon, a headline amount or message, and one line of context. They get
the same head as the transaction receipt, which is what makes the six read as
one family, and they get D-07's buttons and D-08's no-flat-accent rule, which
they currently violate in every file.

The single most important line in this plan is in `swap_drawer_content.dart`:
the amount is painted `Colors.white` on success and `Colors.red` on failure.
That is the exact rule D-03 exists to forbid, and it is the one thing here with
a runnable check.

Purpose: five drawers, one receipt language, zero hard-coded colours.
Output: eight small converted files and the D-03 guard.

**On the file count.** Eight files is above the usual ceiling, and it is
deliberate. The largest is 100 lines, five are under 50, and the change in each
is the same three mechanical moves. Splitting them across plans would mean
re-reading 031-B1 and D-07 three times to make one decision three times - which
is how six files end up with six slightly different receipts. No task in this
plan touches more than four files.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| swap route metadata -> icon URLs | `fromIconUrl` / `toIconUrl` are remote URLs fetched with `Image.network` inside the drawer. |
| chain -> transaction hash | `txHash` and `coinSymbol` are chain-sourced and feed both a rendered string and an explorer URL. |
| explorer URL -> external browser | `launchWebSite` opens a derived URL. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-21-14 | Denial of Service | swap amount / symbol strings in the head | medium | mitigate | `GWDrawerReceiptHead`'s amount and fiat slots ellipsize, inherited from 21-01. An adversarial token symbol from route metadata cannot unbound the drawer. |
| T-21-15 | Information Disclosure | `Image.network` on the icon pair | low | accept | Unchanged from today - the same two URLs are fetched by the same widget. This plan neither adds nor removes a network call, and the drawer renders at most two icons (not a scrolling list). |
| T-21-16 | Tampering | `launchWebSite(context, explorerUrl)` in `swap_result_drawer.dart` | high | accept | Pre-existing: this call site has no `Uri.tryParse` scheme guard, unlike the transaction receipt's. Adding one is a behaviour change in Phase 10's surface and is out of this re-skin's fence. **Filed for follow-up** - record it in the summary as a todo so it is not lost. The footer conversion moves the call verbatim and does not widen the exposure. |
| T-21-17 | Spoofing | result state presentation | medium | mitigate | Removing the red amount could in principle make a failure read as a success. It cannot here: the icon, the pill and (for swaps) the failure sentence all still carry the state, and Task 2's check asserts the pill label differs between the two renders. |

No package-manager installs in this plan.
</threat_model>
