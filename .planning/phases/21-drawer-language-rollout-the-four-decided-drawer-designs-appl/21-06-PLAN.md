caller at once, and delete the last thing that cannot satisfy it.

prove is the phase's actual promise: *every drawer body has the same inset, from
one source, and no drawer is padded twice*. That is a property of the **set** of

is the legacy pre-07-06 shell: its own centred title, a close ✕ on the **left**

inside `ResponsiveDrawer.show()` it is double-padded by construction and paints a
lighter block inside its own drawer — the exact defect
`sdk_account_manager.dart:80-87` documents having escaped from. Measured at HEAD
`08f6df5` it has **exactly one live consumer**: the dev design gallery's drawer
demo. 21-01 recorded it as an unresolved OUTSTANDING item and correctly left it
out of its own file scope. This is where it lands.

Purpose: the invariant, and the deletion that makes it true.

test that fails when the nineteenth drawer forgets.

   This is the **proven repo pattern** for a source-scanning gate that runs
   inside `flutter test`: a `const` in-scope file list derived by hand rather
   than by glob, a `const` allowlist mapping path to an **exact** expected count
   (so deleting an allowlisted occurrence fails just as hard as adding one), and

   applied to drawer insets. Do not invent a second convention.

   `:189-197`, `:321` — `kDrawerBodyPadding`, `kDrawerFooterPadding`, the
   `bodyPadding` parameter and where it is applied. The mechanism is
   **default-padded, `EdgeInsets.zero` to opt out**, which is the inverse of what
   the retired 21-01 plan proposed and the more robust of the two: a forgettable
   opt-in silently regresses, a forgettable opt-out cannot happen.
3. `lib/components/bottom_drawer/bottom_drawer.dart` (95 lines) and
   `lib/dev/design_gallery_screen.dart:865-900`. Confirm for yourself, with a
