<objective>
Build the two boot-path substrates 13-03 assembles: a bound on every CoinGecko request, and the
closing-run timing engine with its runnable check.

Purpose: both belong strictly BEFORE the splash sequencer, not with it or after it.

- The timeout must exist before anything AWAITS a CoinGecko future. 13-03's closing run holds the
  boot screen open until markets and chart settle; with `http.get`'s default of no timeout, a hung
  socket turns D7's "minimum hold" into an unbounded one and the app never leaves the splash. 13-RESEARCH
  frames this as "same commit as the loader removal" (Pitfall 6) — that is the LATEST it may land, not
  the earliest, and landing it in wave 1 is strictly safer.
- The sequencer's timing logic is the one piece of real branching this phase adds (`CLAUDE.md`: non-trivial
  logic leaves ONE runnable check behind). This repo has no working test harness — `flutter test` does not
  compile — so the only way that check can exist is if the logic has no Flutter dependency. Extracting it
  into a plain-Dart `BootSequence` is what makes the check real rather than theatre; contrast, mesh
  behaviour and the frozen-window feel are walk-verified in 13-03/13-05 and are deliberately NOT
  simulated here.

The timeout value is 3 seconds and it is a judgement call, not a measurement — 13-CONTEXT lists it under
Claude's Discretion and 13-RESEARCH logs it as assumption A1. It is written as one named constant so the
number is visible and tunable in one place rather than buried at three call sites. The reasoning: a healthy
CoinGecko round trip is comfortably under a second, so 3s does not fire on a merely-slow-but-alive link and
force an unnecessary fall back to stale cache; and 3s caps a genuinely black-holed socket's cost at roughly
double D7's 1.5s hold rather than at forever. If the walk in 13-05 sees it fire on a healthy connection, or
sees a real hang outlast it, that one constant is the thing to change.

Output: bounded network legs and a tested closing-run engine, both consumed by 13-03.
</objective>
