<objective>
Fix the two boot-path defects that every later plan in this phase stands on, and turn research
assumption A2 into a measured number — before any pixel of the boot screen is touched.

Purpose: two of this phase's five success criteria are decided here, not in the UI.

- **SC4** (zero exceptions / no `LateInitializationError` across a cold start) is E1's single-flight
  guard on `initSDK`, and nothing else in this phase addresses it.
- **SC2** (the dashboard presents complete) depends on 13-04 gating on `coinsStatus`. That gate is
  unsafe against today's `getCoins()`: it dispatches the holdings read with a bare
  `coinFuture.then(...)` and returns immediately, so (a) awaiting `getCoins()` proves nothing and
  (b) a REJECTED read leaves `coinsStatus` on `loading` forever with no `onError` — a widened gate
  on top of that is a permanent dashboard hang, not a nicer boot.

The third job is measurement. 13-RESEARCH's open question 1 / assumption A2 is that the coins leg is
"probably" as fast as the legs spike 002 actually measured (wallets 0ms, transactions 140ms, balances
141ms). It was never in that trace. 13-03 has to decide whether the coins leg can sit inside D7's
1.5s minimum hold or needs its own timeout, and that decision must be made on a number, not a guess.
One instrumented cold start answers both the init-ran-once question and the latency question, which is
why they share a plan and a single walk.

Scope discipline: this plan touches no UI, no theming, no gate and no loader. It is deliberately the
only plan in the phase whose changes are invisible if they work.

Output: a boot path that inits once and reports honest holdings state, plus a measured coins-leg
latency recorded in the SUMMARY for 13-03 to consume.
</objective>
