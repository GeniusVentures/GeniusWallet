Two facts from the brief that ARE still true and still matter:

**Geometry reconciliation.** The sketch says track outer 40 / inner chips 34. In Flutter a
`Container` with `padding: EdgeInsets.all(3)` and `Border.all(width: 1)` adds 4px per side to the
child, so a 34px chip yields a **42px** track. **40 is the number that must hold** (Connect 40, Buy
GNUS 40, one baseline). Therefore inner chips are **32px**: 32 + 6 padding + 2 border = 40.
